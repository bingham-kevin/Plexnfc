# Marquee privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Marquee is an Apple TV app for playing movies from your own Movie Book server on your home network.

## What Marquee collects

Nothing. Marquee has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send any information to the app's developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your Apple TV

- **Your server's address and this Apple TV's name**, so Marquee can reconnect.
- **A pairing secret**, kept in the Apple TV's Keychain. Your server gives it to this Apple TV when you approve its pairing code. It lets the Apple TV ask your server for movies.

Choosing **Disconnect and Forget Server** in Marquee's Settings deletes the pairing secret and this Apple TV's name. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

## Who Marquee talks to

- **Your own Movie Book server**, at the address you enter. Marquee asks it for your library, posters and movie streams, and tells it what's playing so your server's controls (and NFC taps on a printed movie book) work.
- **The media server your Movie Book server uses**, which supplies the movie streams.
- **Public test-stream servers**, only when you play a film in the demo library: Mux (test-streams.mux.dev) and test-videos.co.uk.

These connections stay between your Apple TV and those servers; Marquee's developer never sees them.

## Children

Marquee doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
